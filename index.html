Method, in plain English

ROS total is blended points per game times games with a projection, Weeks 10-17. Bye weeks count as 0. Schedule factor is opponent points allowed to that position, divided by league average, capped 0.85 to 1.15, from actuals Weeks 1-9. Form is 60% last-3 games plus 40% season, and if a player has fewer than 2 games in Weeks 7-9, form falls back to season average. Replacement is the blended points per game at the starter cutoff: QB12, RB24, WR24, TE12 in a 12-team league. Grades are on VOR difference: 40+ A, 25+ B+, 15+ B, 8+ C+, 3+ C, under 3 a wash. Blend weights are a starting point, not backtested. Injury and role change are not in this, beyond what Rotowire already bakes in.

Source: Sleeper API, Rotowire projections. Code and method are in this repo, in scripts/trade_evaluator_v2.py. Raw Sleeper files are not in the repo, they are about 42 MB, re-pull them with scripts/pull_sleeper.py.
