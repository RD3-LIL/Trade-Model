Method, in plain English

ROS total is raw Sleeper points per game times games with a projection, Weeks 10-17. Bye weeks count as 0. Backtest, Weeks 7-9, 692 player-weeks, no leakage: raw Sleeper MAE 4.36, correlation 0.631, best of the methods tried. Schedule-adjusted MAE 4.47, correlation 0.610, worse in every position. Blend with 35% form (60% last-3 plus 40% season) MAE 4.38, correlation 0.619, a wash, and where blend and raw disagreed by 3 or more points, raw was better in 11 of 15 cases. The old blend is still in the repo as an option (--blend in trade_evaluator_v2.py), with form and schedule-adjusted totals shown per player for transparency. Replacement is the raw points per game of the last starter at each position, including the flex: QB12, RB28, WR32, TE12 in a 12-team league, because the 12 flex spots go to 8 WRs and 4 RBs. That is why most players have a negative VOR, only the top 12 QBs, 28 RBs, 32 WRs, and 12 TEs are above replacement. Negative does not mean useless, it means bench or waiver level, and the verdict is on the difference between the two sides, not on whether either side is positive. Grades are on VOR difference: 40+ A, 25+ B+, 15+ B, 8+ C+, 3+ C, under 3 a wash. Top-50 players per week are noisier: raw MAE 6.10, correlation 0.315. Injury and role change are not in this, beyond what Rotowire already bakes in.

Source: Sleeper API, Rotowire projections. Code and method are in this repo, in scripts/trade_evaluator_v2.py. Raw Sleeper files are not in the repo, they are about 42 MB, re-pull them with scripts/pull_sleeper.py.
